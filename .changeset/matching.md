**Matching: prompts matched to a shared list — headings to paragraphs, statements to people, sentence beginnings to endings — with `<Matching>` to draw it**

**Action required:** none for content and grades. Install lk-core 1.13.0 with lk-react 23.10.0. **No grade changes:** every stored grade and validation result replays as before. Two type-level additions can need a line of code: `matching` joins `BUILT_IN_ACTIVITY_TYPES`, `INTERACTIVE_VIDEO_ITEM_TYPES` and `READING_ROUTE_ITEM_TYPES`, and `RedactedMatchingData` joins `RedactedActivity`, so a `switch` or `Record` that is exhaustive over one of them needs a `matching` entry. A host that registered an activity type of its own named `matching` must rename it before upgrading: `registerActivityType` refuses a second descriptor for a built-in type.

- **lk-core:** the `matching` activity type — `MatchingData` with `prompts`, a shared `options` list (each option a `label` such as "A" or "iv", and its text), an `optionsTitle`, `optionUse: 'once' | 'many'` and a seeded `shuffleOptions`. Scored per prompt by option id, `partial` or `all-or-nothing`; an unanswered prompt is `incorrect-omission`. Redaction keeps the list and the prompts and withholds each prompt's `correctOptionId` (`RedactedMatchingData`). Draft checks under `mt_*` codes, four critic findings (`mt_no_distractor` among them), the xAPI `matching` interaction, and frozen grade and validation vectors.
- **lk-core, reading routes:** a matching item's prompts are route questions, one per `partId`. The matching strategies — headings, information, features, sentence endings, multiple matching, gapped text and cross-text — accept it, and their elimination step points at the shared list. Route messages now name a part by its kind: "Gap", "Blank" or "Prompt".
- **lk-core, interactive video:** a video quiz may hold a matching question.
- **lk-react:** `<Matching>` (also `@intellectif/lk-react/components/Matching`): the list under its title, a native selector per prompt, and — with `optionUse: 'once'` — an option picked again moves from the prompt that held it, announced to screen readers. After a check it marks each prompt and shows the right option beside a wrong one where the delivery policy shows answers. `<ActivitySequence>`, `<ActivityPreview>`, `<ReadingRoute>` and `<InteractiveVideo>` draw it, and the sequence's seed guard covers `shuffleOptions`. New strings: `matchPlaceholder`, `matchOptionsLabel`, `matchOptionUsed`, `matchMoved`, `matchCorrectAnswer`. New class hooks: `.lk-mt` and its parts. See https://github.com/intellectif/learning-kit/blob/main/docs/authoring.md#matching--prompts-matched-to-a-shared-list-113
