**Reading routes: guided practice in how to read an IELTS or Cambridge reading passage for its questions, faded from a walkthrough to working alone**

**Action required:** none, unless you implement a complete `LkStrings` of your own (new `route*` keys) or switch over `DraftIssueCode` without a `default` branch (new `ig_route_*` codes). Install lk-core 1.11.0 with lk-react 23.8.0: lk-react calls the new lk-core functions, and its peer range moves with it. A learner's copy of a routed group, redacted by 1.11.0, is refused by an older client's strict `assertRedactedItemGroup`: upgrade clients before servers. **No grade changes:** a route grades nothing, and every question scores as it does without one.

- **lk-core:** an item group may carry a `route`: per question, where the passage justifies the answer (quotes, never offsets), the question's key words with the passage's paraphrase of them, reference links, a word limit and trapped options. Twelve strategies (`ROUTE_STRATEGIES`) cover IELTS True/False/Not Given, Yes/No/Not Given, multiple choice, the matching tasks, completion and summaries, and Cambridge C1/C2 multiple choice, gapped text, multiple matching and cross-text matching. `planRoute` turns a route into the steps a skilled candidate takes; `routeStepText` gives their words. `summarizeRoute` reports, per question, whether the evidence was found and why a wrong answer went wrong (missed the evidence, misread it, a trap, over the word limit); `nextRouteLearnerState` fades help per strategy from `walkthrough` to `coached` to `solo`, and ignores a passage done before. `validateItemGroupDraft` reports 27 `ig_route_*` codes, among them an accepted answer longer than the word limit; `createReadingRouteDraft` starts one. Redaction keeps strategies and removes everything that locates an answer.
- **lk-react:** `<ReadingRoute>` (`@intellectif/lk-react/components/ReadingRoute`) shows the passage beside its questions with a guide above. A walkthrough highlights where to look and why, and asks the learner to choose the sentence before showing it; coached names each step and lets the learner find it; solo answers as in the exam and marks the evidence. An optional suggested time is never enforced. The debrief shows the evidence, numbered paraphrase pairs, the text a trap borrowed from, and each strategy's next level. Keyboard and screen-reader paths throughout; new `--lk-route-*` tokens, light and dark. See https://github.com/intellectif/learning-kit/blob/main/docs/reading-route.md
