Documentation: `<InteractiveVideo>` is a formative tool, for lessons and practice — not for official
summative exams, which `<ActivitySequence>` delivers. The learner controls the recording while the
questions are open (rewinds it, slows it, reads its transcript), a quiz can be reopened, and nothing
in the player times a question or holds a single pass. Its `exam` mode is a check-in without
feedback, not exam conditions; its questions still grade exactly as they do anywhere else.

The component's and `renderMode`'s documentation say so now, as do the README and the interactive
video guide. No behaviour changes.
